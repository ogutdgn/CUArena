# Task 13 — Draw 4 lines (2 vertical + 2 horizontal) forming a hashtag (#) shape.

**Difficulty:** Easy  •  **Time horizon:** 8 min

## Thorough description

Draw 2 vertical lines and 2 horizontal lines, all crossing at right angles to form a hashtag (#) shape. The verticals are parallel to each other; the horizontals are parallel to each other; verticals and horizontals are perpendicular.

## Simplified prompt

> Draw 4 lines (2 vertical + 2 horizontal) forming a hashtag (#) shape.

## Step-by-step

1. Click Line tool. 2. Drag the first vertical line. 3. Right-click then Duplicate, drag right. 4. Click Line tool. 5. Drag the first horizontal line crossing both verticals. 6. Right-click then Duplicate, drag down.
