# Task 46 — Draw 5 vertical rectangles of varying heights sharing a bottom baseline.

**Difficulty:** Easy  •  **Time horizon:** 12 min

## Thorough description

Draw 5 thin vertical rectangles of varying heights placed side-by-side with consistent gap, all sharing a common bottom baseline (like a histogram).

## Simplified prompt

> 5 vertical bars of varying heights, side-by-side, sharing a bottom baseline.

## Step-by-step

1. Click Rectangle tool, drag the first bar. 2. Right-click then Duplicate, drag adjacent right. 3. Scrub height to a different value. 4. Repeat for 5 bars total with varying heights. 5. Marquee all. 6. Click Align bottom.
