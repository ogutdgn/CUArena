# Task 48 — Make a white spiderweb on a navy frame: 4 radial lines + 2 concentric hexagons.

**Difficulty:** Easy  •  **Time horizon:** 12 min

## Thorough description

Inside a navy frame, draw 4 white lines radiating from the center at 90° intervals, plus 2 concentric stroked hexagons (Polygon tool, 6 sides, no fill, white stroke).

## Simplified prompt

> Navy frame + 4 white radial lines (90° apart) + 2 concentric white-stroked hexagons.

## Step-by-step

1. Click Frame tool, navy fill. 2. Click Line tool, draw the first radial line. 3. Right-click then Duplicate, scrub rotation +90°. 4. Repeat for 4 lines total. 5. Click Polygon tool, scrub sides to 6, drag the first hexagon, no fill, white stroke. 6. Right-click then Duplicate, scrub size smaller.
