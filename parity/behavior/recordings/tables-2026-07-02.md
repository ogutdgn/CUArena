Method: computer-use on the interactive desktop (user-approved, Word app only). Fresh blank doc,
typed "Before the table.", drove Insert > Table with real mouse/keyboard, screenshots at each step.
Build: the locked M365 Word-for-Windows 16.0 (ADR-0006). Word closed without saving afterwards.
