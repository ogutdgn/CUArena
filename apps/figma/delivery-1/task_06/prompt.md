# Task 6 — Draw 8 lines from a center point at 45° intervals to form a burst.

**Difficulty:** Easy  •  **Time horizon:** 12 min

## Thorough description

Build an asterisk burst: 8 lines all radiating from a single center point at 45° intervals (so the lines look like a star or a 'splat' burst). Use the line tool. All lines the same color.

## Simplified prompt

> Draw 8 lines from a center point at 45° intervals to form a burst.

## Step-by-step

1. Click Line tool. 2. Drag the first line from center going right. 3. Right-click then Duplicate. 4. Scrub rotation to 45°. 5. Repeat duplicate-and-rotate (90°, 135°, 180°, 225°, 270°, 315°) for 8 lines total.
