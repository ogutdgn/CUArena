# Task 4 — Arrange 6 same-size squares in a hexagonal ring with rainbow colors.

**Difficulty:** Easy  •  **Time horizon:** 12 min

## Thorough description

Inside a frame, arrange 6 same-size squares in a hexagonal ring (3 across the top of an arc and 3 across the bottom, or evenly placed around a hexagon's vertices). Each square filled a different rainbow color: red, yellow, green, cyan, blue, magenta.

## Simplified prompt

> Arrange 6 same-size squares in a hexagonal ring with rainbow colors.

## Step-by-step

1. Click Frame tool. 2. Click Rectangle tool. 3. Drag the first square, pick red. 4. Right-click then Duplicate. 5. Drag duplicate, pick yellow. 6. Repeat duplicate-and-recolor four more times for green, cyan, blue, magenta. 7. Position all 6 in a hexagonal arrangement.
