# Task 43 — Build a compass rose: sand circle + 4 N/E/S/W triangles + gold center.

**Difficulty:** Medium  •  **Time horizon:** 18 min

## Thorough description

Build a compass rose: a sand-colored circle + 4 thin triangles pointing N/E/S/W from center + 1 small gold center circle. The N triangle is red; E/S/W are gray.

## Simplified prompt

> Build a compass rose: sand circle + 4 N/E/S/W triangles + gold center.

## Step-by-step

1. Click Ellipse tool, drag the sand circle. 2. Click Polygon tool, drag a thin triangle pointing up (N), pick red. 3. Right-click then Duplicate, scrub rotation +90° (E), pick gray. 4. Duplicate, +180° (S), gray. 5. Duplicate, +270° (W), gray. 6. Click Ellipse tool, drag a small gold center circle.
