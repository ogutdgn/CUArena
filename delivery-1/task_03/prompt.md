# Task 3 — Draw a yellow center circle and 8 colored petals arranged radially around it.

**Difficulty:** Easy  •  **Time horizon:** 14 min

## Thorough description

Inside a frame, draw 1 yellow center circle and arrange 8 elliptical petals around it in a radial pattern (e.g., one petal on each compass direction plus diagonals). Each petal is a different color.

## Simplified prompt

> Draw a yellow center circle and 8 colored petals arranged radially around it.

## Step-by-step

1. Click Frame tool, drag a frame. 2. Click Ellipse tool. 3. Drag the center circle, pick yellow. 4. Drag a petal ellipse, pick a color. 5. Right-click then Duplicate, rotate 45°, pick a different color. 6. Repeat duplicate-rotate-recolor 6 more times until 8 petals total.
