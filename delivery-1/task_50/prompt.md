# Task 50 — Draw a square with a centered 5-point star on top, contrasting colors.

**Difficulty:** Easy  •  **Time horizon:** 10 min

## Thorough description

Draw a large square + a 5-point star centered on top of the square, with contrasting colors (e.g., dark square + bright star).

## Simplified prompt

> Draw a square with a centered 5-point star on top, contrasting colors.

## Step-by-step

1. Click Rectangle tool, drag the square. 2. Pick a dark fill. 3. Click Star tool, scrub points to 5. 4. Drag the star on top, pick a bright fill. 5. Marquee both. 6. Click Align horizontal centers and Align vertical centers.
